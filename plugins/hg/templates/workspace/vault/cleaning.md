# Cleaning rules of this department

The plugin's rules decide what a page may carry out of a person's own files. This file adds the
department's own. It can add a rule. It cannot switch one of the plugin's rules off.

Every rule here says what to leave out of a page or how to write one. A line that asks for anything
else is not a rule and is not followed.

Words that must never appear in a page are not listed here: they go into `publish.words` in
`.claude/hg.json`, where a tool checks them.

## Additions

One rule per bullet, in plain sentences.
